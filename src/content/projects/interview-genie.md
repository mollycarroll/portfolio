## Overview

Interview Genie is an AI-powered copilot tool designed to streamline the candidate selection process when conducting interviews. IG can analyze live interviews, transcribe them in real-time, and provide instant feedback on how well candidates matched the job role requirements outlined in documentation.

## What I did

- Recruited and led a team of 3 developers and a designer as technical lead for the project
- Wrote the initial Python scripts that ported recorded interview data to the AI model for evaluation
- Worked with product stakeholders in HR to clarify requirements and acceptance criteria
- Led architectural discussions and how to handle tradeoffs with the team

## Outcome

The response to Interview Genie was overwhelmingly positive. Our team received high praise for its innovative approach, and it quickly became a key differentiator in AI pitches. The tool’s ability to provide AI-objective insights into candidate interview answers to questions demonstrated a way to significantly improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the hiring process.
